What folio init made

And who asks for your passphrase.

EnvFolio

Folio init set up a few pieces. Most days, you never think about them.

But when gpg asks for your passphrase, it helps to know who is asking, and for what. Here they are, one at a time.

the pieces · 1 / 8

The store key

In your GPG keyring. Two halves.

EnvFolio

First, a key, in your GPG keyring.

Like every GPG key, it has two halves. The public half encrypts, and checks signatures.

The secret half decrypts, and signs. It is locked by your passphrase.

the pieces · 2 / 8

The store

~/.folio: a pass store, with a git history.

EnvFolio

Next, the store: a folder in your home, dot folio.

It is a plain pass store, with a git history, and only you can read it.

the pieces · 3 / 8

.gpg-id

One line: the fingerprint of the store key.

EnvFolio

In it, the gpg-id file names the key: one line, its fingerprint.

Everything in the store is encrypted to, or signed by, that key.

the pieces · 4 / 8

A secret

Encrypted to the key. Adding one needs no passphrase.

EnvFolio

A secret is a gpg file, encrypted to that key.

Encrypting only needs the public half, so adding a secret needs no passphrase. Reading one does.

the pieces · 5 / 8

A text

Plain, and signed by the key.

EnvFolio

A text is a plain file, signed by the key.

Signing needs the secret half, so adding a text needs the passphrase.

Reading one only checks the signature. No passphrase.

the pieces · 6 / 8

The history

Every change, committed.

EnvFolio

Every change is committed to the store's git history.

Even a removed item is still in it.

the pieces · 7 / 8

The passphrase

gpg asks for it, through pinentry.

EnvFolio

So who asks for your passphrase? Not folio.

gpg does, with a small program called pinentry, in your terminal or in a window.

the pieces · 8 / 8

gpg-agent

Remembers it a while, and unlocks the key.

EnvFolio

Then gpg-agent remembers it for a while, and unlocks the key's secret half.

That is why you are asked once, and then not again for a while.

the pieces · together

folio runs them

pass, gpg and git, for you.

EnvFolio

Folio runs pass, gpg and git for you.

And it is a plain pass store, so pass itself still opens every secret in it. Nothing is locked in.
